**Assumptions**

- Circuit 1 on each link takes the direct great-circle route. Every further circuit takes its own diverse route, bowed out further each time (diverse 1, 2, 3). Each step is 12% of the link distance (3–600 km). In North America the bow goes inland, toward the middle of the continental US, so alternates stay over land.
- Est. route km = geometric path length × 1.3 route factor (fiber slack and rights-of-way).
- One-way ms = route km × 4.9 µs/km (light in single-mode fiber). Propagation only; equipment, queueing and serialization delay are excluded.
- AWS on-ramps run to the assumed region: AWS East = AWS us-east-1, Ashburn, VA; AWS West = AWS us-west-2, Oregon. Internet hand-offs are local and have no path.
